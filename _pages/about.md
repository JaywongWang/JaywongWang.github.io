I am committed to delivering the best media content to billions of people, with cutting-edge content understanding and enhancement technology.

I am currently a senior algorithm engineer at [Tencent Cloud](https://cloud.tencent.com/product/mps), working on <em>**real-world video restoration and super-resolution**</em>, in both livestreaming and offline video scenarios. Our developed media processing services have been widely recognized and applied to many well-known enterprises and products, including but not limited to Douyin(抖音直播), Shipinhao(视频号), Xiaohongshu(小红书), Haiyi(海艺).

I was previously a senior algorithm engineer at [Huya Inc.](https://ir.huya.com/index.php) (2021 ~ 2025), [Bigo](https://www.bigo.tv/) (2021) and a senior research at [Tencent AI Lab](https://ailab.tencent.com/ailab/en/index/) (2018 ~ 2021). My research interest includes <em>**multi-modal understanding and grounding**</em>. 

In 2018, I received my Ph.D. degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China. During 2017 and 2018, I worked as a research intern under the supervision of [Lin Ma](https://forestlinma.com/) in Tencent AI Lab. During 2015 and 2017 I was also a research intern in [Microsoft Research Asia](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-asia-zh-cn/) and worked closely with [Tao Mei](https://taomei.me/) and [Jianlong Fu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-WqSwu8AAAAJ). My research interest includes <em>**video classification, captioning, and grounding**</em>.
