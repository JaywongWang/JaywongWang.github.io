Jingwen Wang

Senior Algorithm Engineer, Tencent Cloud

I am committed to delivering the best media content to billions of people, with cutting-edge content understanding and enhancement technology.

I am currently a senior algorithm engineer at Tencent Cloud, working on real-world video restoration and super-resolution, in both livestreaming and offline video scenarios. Our developed media processing services have been widely recognized and applied to many well-known enterprises and products, including but not limited to Douyin(抖音直播), Shipinhao(视频号), Xiaohongshu(小红书), Haiyi(海艺).

I was previously a senior algorithm engineer at Huya Inc. (2021 ~ 2025), Bigo (2021) and a senior research at Tencent AI Lab (2018 ~ 2021). My research interest includes multi-modal understanding and grounding.

In 2018, I received my Ph.D. degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China. During 2017 and 2018, I worked as a research intern under the supervision of Lin Ma in Tencent AI Lab. During 2015 and 2017 I was also a research intern in Microsoft Research Asia and worked closely with Tao Mei and Jianlong Fu. My research interest includes video classification, captioning, and grounding.
